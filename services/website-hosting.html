Website Design & Hosting

Professional websites for local businesses — designed, hosted, and looked after every month.

Our flagship service: a custom WordPress website for your business, built and hosted by someone local — with a care plan that keeps it fast, updated, and working.

The bigger plans go further: a Job Vault that keeps your contracts and job photos organized and lawsuit-proof, AI that qualifies your leads in seconds, and real-time alerts so you never miss a hot lead while you're on the job.

What's included

  • Custom WordPress site, designed for your business
  • Hosting, security, backups, and updates included
  • Job Vault — contracts and job photos, organized by job
  • AI lead qualification and real-time alerts (Flagship plans)
  • Support from someone local, by phone or email
